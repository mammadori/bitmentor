# Contributing

Thank you for helping. bitmentor is distributed under the AGPLv3, and its authors also license it under other terms on
request ([COPYING.md](COPYING.md)). To keep that possible, every contribution comes with this agreement.

**By sending a contribution** (a patch, a pull request, a commit, a translation, a document) you declare that:

1. you wrote it, or you have the right to submit it, and it is not covered by terms that conflict with these;
2. you license it under the AGPLv3, as the rest of bitmentor;
3. you also grant Marco Amadori, the maintainer, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free and irrevocable
   license to use, modify and distribute it, and to license it to others under any terms, including other open-source
   and commercial licenses;
4. you keep your own copyright, and you may use your contribution as you wish.

Sign every commit with `git commit -s` (a `Signed-off-by:` line): it says you agree to the above for that commit.

Write in English (code, comments, messages, documents); translations go in `locale/`. No community's name in the
software: themes, texts and data live in the community package (`COMMUNITY_DIR`).
