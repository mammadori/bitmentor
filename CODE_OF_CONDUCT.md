# Code of conduct

This is software, not a primary school.

- We are adults. Behave like one, and assume the others are too.
- Talk about the code, the bug or the idea. Who wrote it is not a topic.
- Nobody owes you an answer, a fix or a feature. A good patch goes further than a loud opinion.
- Being offended is not an argument, and neither is being rude. The first gets no reply; the second, when it keeps
  up, gets you shown the door.
- The maintainer decides what goes in and who stays. There is no committee, no appeal and no form to fill in.

That is all. If you need more rules than these to work with other people, more rules are not what you are missing.
